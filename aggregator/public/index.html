Local metadata storage

This aggregator stores public directory metadata, ratings, and operation records. Article bodies are not written to disk.

Listings and ratings remain in the local discovery window for 30 days; popularity uses ratings from the last 7 days. Journal entries expire after 7 days. Each budget evicts the oldest records. Article files have no client age expiry; this metadata index does not certify their availability.
