Dashboard storage

This dashboard stores public titles, links, ratings, and its activity log. It does not store article contents.

Listings and ratings are kept for 30 days. Popularity uses the last 7 days of ratings. Activity is kept for 7 days. Older records are removed when storage fills up. An article listed here may no longer be available.
